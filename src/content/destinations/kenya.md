The East African country is located halfway down the east coast of Africa, astride the equator. With
over 54 national parks and game reserves, a sheer diversity of wildlife, birdlife and cultures, and an
excellent network of international flights arriving and departing every day of the week, Kenya is an
easy and fascinating destination every holidaymaker should put on their bucket list.

Kenya is the best place to go on safari: enthusiasts never miss an opportunity to encounter wildlife
coexisting in its truest natural habitat. It is also an ornithological paradise for bird lovers, with
magnificent beaches that have won accolades across the globe and a rich history with plenty of
monuments and museums to show for it. Better still, the warm weather all year round means you can
visit any time of the year.

With over 42 ethnic communities across 47 counties, Kenyans proudly embrace their individual cultures
and traditions while recognising the importance of national solidarity, captured in the motto
“Harambee”, Swahili for “pulling together”.
