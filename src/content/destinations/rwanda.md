Welcome to *le Pays des Mille Collines*, the Land of a Thousand Hills.

Rwanda is a landlocked country on the eastern rim of the Albertine Rift, the western arm of the Great
Rift Valley. Much of the country is attractively mountainous, its green, undulating hills dotted with
gardens and tea plantations. It is home to around a third of the world’s remaining mountain gorillas,
a third of Africa’s bird species and several other species of primates.

The capital, Kigali, is an attractive city sprawling across mountain ridges and the valleys between.

## Gorilla trekking

Volcanoes National Park lies in the Virunga Mountains, a magnificent chain of dormant volcanoes in the
north of the country. As well as mountain gorillas, it is home to rare golden monkeys, Cape buffalo and
a profusion of exotic birds. Gorilla tracking is the main attraction, but you can also visit the grave
of Dian Fossey, who studied gorillas in Rwanda for many years, explore caves and track golden monkeys.

Trekking can be reasonably strenuous — up to six hours at relatively high altitude — but the experience
will last a lifetime.
